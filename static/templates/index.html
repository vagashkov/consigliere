Consigliere Chat Widget Demo

What is Consigliere?

Consigliere is a multichannel personal AI assistant that can be integrated into any website or most common messengers.

Deployment

To deploy chat widget to your site add just one line of code:

                
                    <script src="https://your-server.com/static/js/chat_widget.js"
                    data-api="https://your-server.com/api/v1"
                    data-theme="light"
                    data-position="bottom-right"
                    data-title="Consigliere">
                    </script>

Enjoy :)

Click the chat button in the bottom-right corner to start communicating with Consigliere.
